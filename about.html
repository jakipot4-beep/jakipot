Our Beginning
EST. 2024 Born in the United Kingdom 🇬🇧

Jakipot began in 2024, founded by an individual from the United Kingdom, with a vision of creating something meaningful — a digital platform where people could connect, discover opportunities, and become part of a growing community.

The Vision

"Opportunities become greater when people come together."

Jakipot was created with a purpose: to build a platform that brings people from different backgrounds, communities, and countries together.

A place where users can discover, connect, share, and explore different opportunities through one digital experience.

A New Chapter
15 SEPTEMBER 2026 JAKIPOT WIN — Africa 🌍
⭐ MILESTONE
🌍 A Growing Presence in Africa

On 15 September 2026, Jakipot became known as JAKIPOT WIN, marking a new chapter in its journey and its growing presence in Africa.

This milestone represents the beginning of a broader vision — connecting people across Africa and beyond while welcoming a global community.
