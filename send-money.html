Free Transfers

Send Money For Free 🇿🇲

Learn how to send money to your friends and family across Zambia without paying transfer fees using your mobile money network.

MTN

MTN MoMo

Zero fees for same-network transfers

1
Dial *303# on your phone and press call.
2
Select option 1. Send Money from the menu.
3
Enter the recipient's MTN phone number (e.g., 096/076).
4
Enter the amount you want to send. Check if the fee shows K0.00 before confirming.
5
Enter your MTN MoMo PIN to authorize the transfer.

Important: MTN often waives fees for transactions under a certain amount (e.g., K50 or K100) or for same-network transfers. Always review the confirmation screen to ensure you are not charged a fee.

Airtel

Airtel Money

Affordable & free options

1
Dial *115# on your phone and press call.
2
Select option 1. Send Money.
3
Enter the recipient's Airtel phone number (e.g., 097/077).
4
Enter the amount. Airtel sometimes offers free transfers for specific amounts or promotions.
5
Enter your Airtel Money PIN to authorize.

Important: Airtel Money frequently runs "Zero Fee" promotions for specific transaction brackets (e.g., sending K10 to K100). Watch out for the SMS confirmation showing the exact fee before you input your PIN.
